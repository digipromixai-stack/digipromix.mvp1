Competitive-intelligence crawler · operated by DigiPromix AI · last updated May 25, 2026

User-AgentDigiPromix-Bot/2.x (+https://www.digipromix.com/bot)

What it does

DigiPromix-Bot fetches public, non-authenticated web pages on behalf of our customers to detect competitive market signals: price changes, new promotions, new landing pages, banner refreshes, and campaign launches.

What it does NOT do

  • It never logs in, never bypasses paywalls, never accesses anything behind authentication.
  • It never submits forms, never clicks Buy / Add-to-cart, never abuses search.
  • It never collects personal data (names, emails, account info, reviews with author info).
  • It never republishes scraped content — only short factual summaries flow to our customers.

How it behaves

  • Honors your robots.txt — paths you disallow are skipped automatically.
  • Identifies itself in the User-Agent header.
  • Rate-limited to once every 1–6 hours per page.
  • Retains snapshots for a maximum of 90 days (then auto-purged).

How to block us

Add this to your /robots.txt:

User-agent: DigiPromix-Bot
Disallow: /

We re-fetch robots.txt every 24 hours, so changes take effect within a day.

Takedown / opt-out

If you'd prefer email contact or want a faster response, email abuse@digipromix.com with the affected domain. We honor takedown requests within 24 hours and confirm by reply.

Verification

DigiPromix-Bot crawls from an AWS EC2 instance in us-east-1 (IP 44.222.116.245) for most fetches, and from rotating commercial proxy IPs (scrape.do) for sites with anti-bot defenses. If you see this User-Agent from elsewhere it isn't us — please report it.
